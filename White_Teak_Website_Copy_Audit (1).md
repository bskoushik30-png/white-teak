## Detailed Corrections

### Hero section

**Current:**\
"A haven in the heart of Gokulam where the art of specialty coffee meets
the craft of soulful food --- weaving a tapestry of unhurried, all-day
cafe moments."

**Recommended:**\
"A haven in the heart of Gokulam, where the art of specialty coffee
meets the craft of soulful food --- creating unhurried, all-day café
moments."

### Our Menu

**Current:**\
"Every plate and every cup on our menu is a small ceremony --- made
slow, with local ingredients and a lot of heart."

**Recommended:**\
"Every plate and every cup on our menu is a small ceremony --- made
slowly, with local ingredients and a lot of heart."

### Escape · Breathe · Savour

**Current:**\
"Savor"

**Recommended:**\
"Savour". Standardise the site to British/Indian English.

### Our Story

**Current:**\
"Filtered with Love": being the foundation for our all-day cafe born out
of a mutual love for specialty coffee and craft food.

**Recommended:**\
""Filtered with Love" is the foundation of our all-day café --- born
from a mutual love for specialty coffee and craft food."

### The Coffee

**Current:**\
"Along with being our founding-planter, we have chosen to work in direct
trade relationships at origin with coffee estates and smallholder
farmers, harvesting and processing coffees that we aim to curate into
only SCA 85+ point scoring coffees."

**Recommended:**\
"Along with being our founding planter, we work directly with coffee
estates and smallholder farmers at origin, sourcing and processing
coffees that we carefully curate into a selection of SCA 85+
point-scoring coffees."

## Recommended Master Copy

> "Filtered with Love" is the foundation of our all-day café --- born
> from a mutual love for specialty coffee and craft food.
>
> Along with being our founding planter, we work directly with coffee
> estates and smallholder farmers at origin, sourcing and processing
> coffees that we carefully curate into a selection of SCA 85+
> point-scoring coffees.
>
> We take pride in introducing White Teak Cascara --- a coffee cherry
> tea made from the dried skins of coffee cherries, brewed much like a
> specialty tea.
>
> We source teas directly from farmers and craft them into unique blends
> in small batches. Freshness matters to us, so the menu stays focused
> and deliberate instead of overextended.
>
> We bring together people from all walks of life through specially
> curated events that celebrate coffee, craft, design, and conversation.
>
> Immerse yourself in the tranquillity of nature at our café.

## Note

The audit focuses on the currently accessible website copy and flags
both objective errors and optional copy improvements. Product claims
such as "100% natural" and SCA scoring language should be fact-checked
against the brand's actual product standards before publication.
